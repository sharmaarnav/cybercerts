About CyberCerts

Your comprehensive guide to navigating the cybersecurity certification landscape.

🎯

Our Mission

CyberCerts was built to help security professionals at every stage of their career make informed decisions about which certifications to pursue. With over 170 certifications across 13 domains, the landscape can be overwhelming — we make it navigable.

📊

What We Cover

  • 170+ certifications across 13 security domains
  • All major issuers: ISC2, ISACA, CompTIA, EC-Council, GIAC/SANS, OffSec, and more
  • Emerging AI Security certifications
  • ICS/OT, Privacy, Cloud, and vendor-specific paths
  • Salary data and career role mapping
🛣️

How to Use CyberCerts

  1. Discover: Use the 3-step finder on the homepage to get personalized recommendations
  2. Explore: Browse and filter all certifications by domain, level, and issuer
  3. Plan: Build your visual pathway and track your progress
  4. Deep Dive: View detailed pages for each certification with exam info, salary data, and resources
⚠️

Disclaimer

Certification details including exam costs, question counts, and passing scores change frequently. Always verify current information directly with the certification issuer before registering for an exam. Salary ranges are approximate figures based on general market data.
